Cardhop-style contact manager for macOS 15+, built on the system Contacts database (iCloud, Google, Exchange and local accounts).

- **One-line entry**: `Jane Doe, CEO at Acme Inc jane@acme.com 415 555 0100 @janed bday Apr 12` becomes a contact; `call Jane` / `email Jane` act on one. Available from the menu bar too.
- **Duplicate detection**: normalized emails and phones, nicknames, initials, swapped names and typos, with safeguards against households and shared mailboxes. Groups are rated High / Medium / Possible and show why they matched.
- **Merge**: side-by-side review with a preview of the merged result. Creating a contact that already exists offers "Add to…" instead.
- **Auto-merge** (off by default): merges only groups where every pair shares an email, phone or social profile and clears a strict score.
- **Merge History with Undo**: every merge and delete is backed up to a vCard first.
